To capture stills:

1. Open the combined environment and wait for the editor to finish loading. Keep local inference unloaded while Unreal runs.
2. Run `Capture_Thermal_Angles.cmd`. It uses the already open combined map, starts Play if needed, and takes one batch. It never switches maps or launches another editor.
3. Each run creates `Saved/SceneDiversity/viewpoint_runs/<date_time>/`. It contains paired RGB/IR PNGs, per-pair metadata, `manifest.json`, `audit.json` and a paired contact sheet. Nothing records automatically while you explore the environment.
4. To take a smaller batch, run `Capture_Thermal_Angles.cmd --count 10`. This selects the first ten configured views. To change the selection, copy/edit the explicit view list and run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe Scripts\SceneDiversity\capture_angles.py --views <your.json> --output <empty-folder>` while Play is running.

Edit **`Config/SceneDiversity/viewpoints.json`** for camera positions and targets. Coordinates are Unreal centimetres. Every entry is an independent viewpoint with an ID and zone; the camera teleports to that position, aims at its target, settles, and saves one RGB/IR pair. It does not interpolate a route or record intervening frames. The default 50 views cover close front/side/rear views and elevated/wider compositions, distributed 13/13/12/12 across the four areas. Three camera heights were adjusted against actual visibility collision. Camera clearance is checked again at capture time; recheck visibility after changing the scene.

The batch suspends scripted vehicle movement and temperature curves for the whole collection. Each pair is read in one API call while the simulation is paused; between views, the world runs briefly to settle rendering. Native surface heat can continue settling during those intervals. Camera pose, timestamp and fixed source-state checks are recorded per pair. After capture the previous camera, pause and script-suspension settings are restored.

The verified example is **`Saved/SceneDiversity/thermalgen_angles50/`**, containing matching filenames in `rgb/`, `ir/` and `metadata/`, plus `pairs.csv`. The gallery is **`Docs/SceneDiversity/Combined/INDEX.html`**. These are 8-bit display-mapped thermal PNGs, not calibrated radiometric temperatures. This is a small synthetic sample collection; avoid randomly splitting angles of the same objects/background across training and evaluation sets. Split by independently varied scenes/assets/states for meaningful evaluation.

For manual scene edits, filter the Outliner by `Combined_cafe`, `Combined_service`, `Combined_market` or `Combined_parking`. Their vehicle/fixture folders identify each area. Change operating-state and lighting presets in the `05_combined_environment` entry in `Config/SceneDiversity/scenes.json`; restart Play to apply them. Moving camera viewpoints does not require rebuilding the map. Do not rerun map generation over an edited map.
